📜 Server Rules

Clear, fair, and made to keep the community fun.
📜 Channel Rules Be cool
  • 🤝
    Be Respectful
    No harassment, hateful language, or targeted attacks. Disagree without being a jerk.
  • 🔇
    No Spamming
    No rapid messages, repeated images, or excessive emoji. Threads for long chats.
  • 📌
    Use the Right Channel
    Read channel topics: memes ➜ #memes, help ➜ #support, art ➜ #showcase.
  • 🚫
    No NSFW / Illegal Content
    Sexual, graphic, or illegal content is not allowed anywhere here.
  • 📢
    No Unsolicited Advertising
    No server drops or DM ads. Ask staff if you want to promote something.
  • ⚠️
    No Self-Harm Encouragement
    Content that encourages self-harm is not allowed — seek help and contact mods if you see it.
  • 🔍
    Spoiler Etiquette
    Use spoiler tags for plot points and major reveals — be considerate.
🎧 Voice Chat Rules Keep it chill
  • 🎙️
    Push-to-Talk or Mute
    Keep background noise low and mute when away.
  • 🔊
    Respect Volume
    No ear-rape or sudden loud audio; check your mic before joining.
  • 🎥
    Ask Before Recording
    Always get consent before recording or streaming others.
🌟 Extra Rules Community first
  • ❤️
    Be Kind
    Small kindnesses matter. Avoid microaggressions and gatekeeping.
  • 🔒
    Protect Privacy
    No doxxing or sharing private info without consent.
  • 👑
    Respect Staff
    If staff asks something, comply or appeal privately. Abuse of staff is not tolerated.
  • 🧭
    Follow Discord TOS
    We follow Discord's Terms of Service — violations may result in removal.

📜 Server Rules

Short, clear, and fair — follow them and people will thank you.
📜 Channel Rules Enforced
  • 🤝
    Be respectful (basic)
    No insults, targeted harassment, or hateful slurs. Disagreements are fine — personal attacks are not.
  • 🔇
    No spamming or flooding
    Repeated messages, giant walls of text, or excessive emoji are not allowed. Keep conversations readable.
  • 📌
    Post in the correct channel
    Memes in #memes, help in #support — follow channel descriptions or staff guidance.
  • 🚫
    No NSFW / sexual content
    This server is safe for a broad audience. Sexual or explicit content will be removed and may result in a ban.
  • 📢
    No advertising without permission
    DM ads, server drops, or unsolicited links are prohibited. Ask staff for promo rules.
  • 🗣️
    Language & clarity
    Use English in main chat unless a channel says otherwise. Be clear — avoid excessive shorthand that confuses others.
  • 🔔
    Don't ping excessively
    Avoid @everyone/@here and repeated pings. Use DMs when appropriate.
  • 🛑
    Let staff moderate
    If you have an issue with a user, report it to staff. Do not take moderation into your own hands.
  • 📜
    Follow Discord Terms
    Illegal activity and TOS violations will be reported to Discord. Read Discord Community Guidelines.
  • 💬
    Use spoilers & threads appropriately
    Spoilers must use spoiler tags; keep long side chats to threads to reduce clutter.
🎧 Voice Chat Rules Voice
  • 🎙️
    Use push-to-talk or mute when not speaking
    Keep background noise to a minimum; mute when away.
  • 🔊
    Respect volume levels
    No ear-rape audio, sudden loud sounds, or music at high volumes.
  • 🗯️
    Don't interrupt
    Allow others to speak; avoid talking over people or creating a hostile environment.
  • 🎵
    Music only in dedicated VC
    Use the music channel for DJing/streaming; ask before sharing audio in other VCs.
  • 🎥
    No recording without consent
    Always ask — recording without permission is strictly forbidden.
  • 👮‍♂️
    Follow staff directions
    If staff asks you to mute/leave, do so politely — appeals can be made via DM.
🌟 Extra Server Rules General
  • ❤️
    Be kind and inclusive
    Treat others how you'd like to be treated; microaggressions aren't allowed.
  • 🎁
    Giveaways: fair play
    No farming or fake accounts; winners chosen per giveaway rules.
  • 👑
    Respect staff decisions
    If you disagree, appeal calmly — do not publicly harass staff.
  • 🎭
    No impersonation
    Do not impersonate staff, creators, or other users to deceive others.
  • 🔒
    Protect privacy
    No doxxing or sharing private info without consent.
  • 😄
    Have fun — responsibly
    We want a fun server — but don't let fun hurt someone else.
